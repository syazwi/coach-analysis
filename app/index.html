Recording tips

  • Film from behind the baseline, raised 2–3 m, centered, whole court in frame.
  • 60 fps, 1080p, tripod or fence mount — no hand-held.
  • If teams switch ends, split them into separate games in Game time and analyze each part separately.

Identify the players

Pick a frame where all players are visible and standing apart (ideally near the start of the analysis range). Select a player on the right, then click their skeleton.

The shirt colour sampled here is used to keep each player's identity when partners cross or someone is briefly hidden. If a player isn't detected, click on their feet to place them manually.

Calibrate the ball

Press ▶ (slow motion helps) and pause when the ball is clearly visible, then click its centre. Use ◀/▶ frame to fine-tune. Set the size so the ring matches the ball. Add 2–3 samples from different spots (sun / shade, near / far) for best results.
